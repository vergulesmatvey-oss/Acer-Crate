# Laptop artwork

The catalog contains 38 bundled photographs from Acer's public product catalog and official press releases. Original page and image URLs are recorded per entry in `catalog.json`.

Product photographs retain their original Acer copyright and trademarks; they identify devices and are separate from the GPL-licensed application code. Configurations, finishes and regional variants may differ from a promotional image.

The app first matches the exact model code. Other codes within an available series use a series illustration labelled as such. A bundled photograph does not certify firmware compatibility. The offline photo selector allows manual choice; automatic model detection remains the default.
